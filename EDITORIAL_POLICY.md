# Sensitive content & editorial policy

- Emphasize emotional validation and non-diagnostic self-reflection, not confirmation that someone's partner has a disorder.
- Distinguish personal first-person testimony, fictional illustrations, professional clinical evidence and editorial interpretation.
- Founder shared these therapy experiences: schematherapie (self discovery), group schematherapie, EMDR (found insufficient personally, then exposure), cognitive therapy (handling thoughts), boxing with emotions (feeling how much energy emotions cost), visual arts therapy (uncovering internal wants), psychologist, meditation. Do NOT invent efficacy, chronology, dates or insights for unelaborated therapies.
- Childhood abuse history may be referenced only with author consent and no invented detail/causal claims.
- Father's letter to his children must say he loves them deeply and feels joy/pride, without positioning them as responsible for his survival or mental health. Do not identify them publicly.
- Currently it is one person's initiative and story; no 'wij' implying team, no open call for submitted stories. Future possibility only.
- Dutch crisis contacts need current verification and country scope. A person in immediate danger should contact emergency services; support resources are not a substitute for professional care.
- “Mannen in cijfers” only with exact dated and sourced population/methodology; suicide prevalence by gender is not evidence of suicide being caused by abuse or loneliness.
