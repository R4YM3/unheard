Unheard is a website with explanations and cards. There is no helpline here, no therapist and no confidential conversation. The organisations below do offer help. You do not need a label first. Doubt is a valid reason to call.

This site is based in the Netherlands. The services on this page are Dutch.

Men sometimes put off making a call, out of shame or because they feel they ought to sort it out themselves. The lines here are there for them too, and for everyone.

The Dutch numbers on this page were last checked on 20 October 2026, on each organisation's official website. The sections for readers outside the Netherlands and on help in English were checked on 4 November 2026.

## In immediate danger

Call [112](tel:112) in immediate danger or when someone's life is at risk: violence that is happening now, a medical emergency, or a moment when you or someone else is not safe. You do not have to explain it perfectly. The 113 helpline also says: "Is your life in danger? Call 112 straight away." If you are unsure whether it is serious enough while there is danger right now, 112 is still the right step. This website is not.

## If you are not in the Netherlands

The numbers on this page are Dutch. 113, for example, can only be reached [from within the Netherlands](https://www.113.nl/english). Somewhere else, call the local emergency number:

- **112** works [everywhere in the European Union, free of charge](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/112). In the Netherlands it is the only emergency number.
- **999** is the emergency number in the United Kingdom; [112 works there too](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/999-and-112-the-uks-national-emergency-numbers).
- **911** in the [United States](https://www.911.gov/) and Canada.
- **000** in [Australia](https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/media-communications/phone/triple-zero).

Want advice from Veilig Thuis while you are abroad? According to the [Dutch government](https://www.government.nl/documents/question-and-answer/im-a-victim-of-honour-based-violence-how-can-i-get-help), you can call +31 70 311 9007 (phone charges apply).

## Help in English

Not every service on this page says whether it can help in English. What we could check:

- **113** writes that it can usually [help in English as well](https://www.113.nl/heb-je-nu-hulp-nodig/hulplijn): call or chat, and ask for someone who speaks English.
- **The police** say that if you find Dutch difficult, you can [ask for an interpreter](https://www.politie.nl/en/information/as-a-victim-of-a-crime-you-have-rights.html), free of charge, for example when you report a crime.
- **Slachtofferhulp Nederland** (Victim Support Netherlands) has an [English page](https://www.slachtofferhulp.nl/english/). Its website is in Dutch.
- For **Veilig Thuis** we found no statement about help in English. You can still call and ask.

## If your residence permit depends on your partner

If you live in the Netherlands on a residence permit to stay with a family member, and you are a victim of violence within the family, the IND (the Dutch immigration service) describes when you can [apply for a residence permit of your own](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/other-residence-permits/residence-permit-honour-based-or-domestic-violence). The rules are detailed. Ask for advice before you apply, for example through Veilig Thuis or a lawyer.

## With thoughts of suicide

Thoughts of suicide are a reason to reach out, even without a plan and even if you cannot explain it yet. This page does not draw a link between domestic violence and suicide.

You can call [113 Zelfmoordpreventie](https://www.113.nl/heb-je-nu-hulp-nodig/hulplijn) (the Dutch suicide prevention helpline) free of charge and anonymously on [113](tel:113), 24 hours a day. You can chat via 113.nl. The chat is open day and night.

Since 3 October 2025, the short number 113 itself has been free. [0800-0113](tel:08000113) remains available as a free alternative for the same helpline.

If your life is in danger, call [112](tel:112) straight away. Shame does not have to hold you back, even if as a man you learned to keep this to yourself. One sentence is enough.

## With worries about domestic violence or abuse

[Veilig Thuis](https://www.veiligthuis.nl/nl/contact) (the Dutch advice and reporting centre for domestic violence and child abuse) is there for everyone dealing with domestic violence or child abuse, including if you are a man. You call [0800-2000](tel:08002000), free, anonymous and day and night. There is also a chat; how to reach it and its opening hours are on the Veilig Thuis contact page.

The [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/recht-veiligheid-en-defensie/huiselijk-geweld/vraag-en-antwoord/huiselijk-geweld/wat-moet-ik-doen-bij-huiselijk-geweld) (the Dutch government) explains what you can do if you suspect domestic violence. You do not have to prove it. Doubt is enough to call. You can describe emotional pressure or control through money the way you experience it. Unheard does not decide whether it "counts".

Ask what will and will not be shared afterwards. This site does not promise that a conversation stays invisible. If you are worried about someone else, read [I am worried about someone](nl/ik-maak-me-zorgen-om-iemand/) (in Dutch).

## Other help

Not everything is an emergency. The options below do not replace 112 or Veilig Thuis.

### Huisarts and praktijkondersteuner

With mental health problems, the huisarts (GP, your family doctor) is a place to start. With low mood, the praktijkondersteuner (POH-GGZ, a mental health support worker at the GP practice) can help. With stress that has lasted a long time, often because of uncertainty about money, family or a relationship, [Thuisarts](https://www.thuisarts.nl/stress/ik-heb-al-heel-lang-stress) (a Dutch website) points to the huisarts or the praktijkondersteuner. [Thuisarts](https://www.thuisarts.nl/geweld-door-iemand-die-je-kent/partner-ex-partner-mishandelt-mij) also describes what you can do if a partner or ex abuses you, and what role the huisarts can play in that.

An appointment is not an emergency line. You decide what you share. There is a memory aid in [preparing for a conversation with your GP or therapist](nl/hulpmiddelen/voorbereiding-gesprek-huisarts-of-therapeut/) (in Dutch).

### Slachtofferhulp Nederland

[Slachtofferhulp Nederland](https://www.slachtofferhulp.nl/) (the name means Victim Support Netherlands) can be reached on [0900-0101](tel:09000101). This number is not free, and it does not replace 112 or Veilig Thuis. What they can do in a specific situation is explained on their website.

### Het Juridisch Loket

[Het Juridisch Loket](https://www.juridischloket.nl/contact/) gives free legal advice to people on a low income with little savings, on working days from 09:00 to 17:00, on [0800-8020](tel:08008020). The information on their website is there for everyone. Unheard does not give legal advice itself. Do not call this number in immediate danger.

### Geldfit

For questions and worries about money, you can call [Geldfit](https://geldfit.nl/) on [0800-8115](tel:08008115). Costs and opening hours are on their website. When advice can be useful, without steps that bring risk in an unsafe situation, is explained in [legal and financial advice](nl/begrijp-het/financieel-misbruik/juridisch-en-financieel-advies/) (in Dutch).

## Searching safely and shared devices

Someone who shares your phone or computer can see the screen, open tabs, the history, and suggestions when you start typing an address. A shared account can sync history, photos or documents. Location sharing, in an app or through a family account, can show where you are. Turning it off can be noticed. Do not do that on your own if you are afraid of that.

A private window limits what the browser keeps in its normal history afterwards. That is a limitation, not cover. Someone looking over your shoulder sees the screen. Your internet provider, a work or school network, the hosting of a site and software on the device can still see a connection. Downloads can stay on the device.

Changing passwords or logging out can also be noticed. Do not do that if you fear the reaction. Ask for advice first, for example from Veilig Thuis. No website can erase traces for you. Unheard cannot either, and this site does not promise confidentiality. There is more in [safe use](en/safe-use/).

## Printing and paper

A printer can keep a queue or a history. A sheet can be left lying in the tray, at home or at work.

The cards in [tools](nl/hulpmiddelen/) (in Dutch) are designed for A4, with blank lines. What you type stays in your browser, is not saved or sent, and does not appear on the printout by default. Only if you choose to print your answers will they be on paper. Close the tab when you are done. Some browsers restore text when you go back to a page. Keep paper somewhere that is yours, or throw it away.

## When it is better not to confront

Wanting clarity is understandable. Even so, a conversation can be unsafe. Safety matters more than being proved right.

If you are afraid, do not start a conversation to get a confession, and do not start a confrontation to prove you are right. The [questions for a conversation with your partner](nl/hulpmiddelen/vragen-voor-een-gesprek-met-je-partner/) (in Dutch) are only for when it feels safe. You do not have to have that conversation at all. Never use that card with someone you are afraid of.

Do not secretly collect evidence if being found out could raise the tension. Do not change shared accounts, locks or passwords as long as you do not know whether that is safe. Ask for advice first. There is a checklist to help with that choice in the [financial overview](nl/hulpmiddelen/financieel-overzicht-en-veiligheid/) (in Dutch).

## Confiding in someone

One person who can listen is sometimes enough: a friend, a sister, a colleague. Say whether you want them to listen, give advice, or do something. You do not have to tell everything straight away.

For many men, asking for help feels like failing. That does not make the experience any smaller. If the first person brushes it off, you can choose someone else.

Here, a person you confide in means someone you trust. Unheard does not appoint anyone and cannot fill that role. If there is no one, the numbers above are there. One phone call is a valid next step.
