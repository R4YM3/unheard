## Why start with a feeling?

Many people first look for a word for what the other person is doing. That makes sense: you want to know what is going on. But often the first thing you know for sure is not what the other person means, but what it does to you. You doubt yourself more. You feel guilty without knowing why. You are tired of always being on your guard.

That is why Unheard starts with you. Each feeling page describes a feeling, what may be happening around you, what might explain it and what can help. It includes fictional examples and questions you can answer if you want to, with no obligation.

## English pages so far

In English there is one feeling page for now: [I doubt myself](en/what-do-i-feel/i-doubt-myself/). The other feelings are available in Dutch for the time being. You can find them on [Wat voel ik?](nl/wat-voel-ik/) (in Dutch).

## What these pages don’t do

These pages do not make a diagnosis, not about you and not about anyone else. They also do not say that something is abuse if you recognise it. Feelings can have many causes, and relationships are complicated. What they do: give you words, so you can explore what is going on and who you want to talk to about it.

Unheard pays special attention to men, because for them it often feels extra hard to talk about this. But everyone is welcome, whatever their gender or type of relationship.

Want to learn more about patterns first? Read [What is emotional abuse?](en/understand/emotional-abuse/) or see [Begrijp het](nl/begrijp-het/) (in Dutch). Do you need help now? Go to [Find help](en/find-help/).
