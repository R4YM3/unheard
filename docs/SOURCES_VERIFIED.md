## Statistics (only for `/nl/mannen-in-cijfers/`)

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| In 2024, 4,9% of men and 6,9% of women aged 16+ reported psychological violence in the domestic sphere in the previous 12 months (total 5,9%, ~870,000 people). "Psychisch geweld" = structural (monthly or more) verbal aggression, plus forms of coercive control with threat and intimidation, by someone from the domestic sphere — broader than partners. New concept in 2024; earlier years re-derived. | [CBS/WODC — Prevalentiemonitor Huiselijk Geweld en Seksueel Grensoverschrijdend Gedrag 2024, hoofdstuk 2](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/longread/rapportages/2024/prevalentiemonitor-huiselijk-geweld-en-seksueel-grensoverschrijdend-gedrag-2024/2-psychisch-geweld-in-huiselijke-kring) (published 25-11-2024) |
| Of victims of psychological domestic violence, 73% of men and 86% of women talked to someone about it. Overall 81% talked to someone, 15% to no one; about a third (32,6%) talked to a care professional (GP, psychologist, social worker); 2,5% to Veilig Thuis, 3,9% to police. "Talked to someone" ≠ professional help ≠ police report. | Same chapter, §2.4 |
| Method: online survey; random sample of 100,000 people aged 16+ registered in the BRP in private households (institutions excluded); fieldwork March–April 2024; self-report. | [CBS — Bijlage B Onderzoeksverantwoording](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/longread/rapportages/2024/prevalentiemonitor-huiselijk-geweld-en-seksueel-grensoverschrijdend-gedrag-2024/bijlage-b-onderzoeksverantwoording) |
| In 2025 (provisional), 1,205 men and 553 women died by suicide (1,758 total; 9,7 per 100,000). Suicide among men is about twice as common. These figures show **no** causal link with abuse, separation or loneliness. | [CBS — 1 758 zelfdodingen in 2025](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/25/1-758-zelfdodingen-in-2025-gemiddeld-bijna-5-per-dag) (19-06-2026) |
